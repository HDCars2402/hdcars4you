Full Rental Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2026 • HDCars4You (Fraser Rise, VIC 3336)

Browse Fleet & Book

1. Driver Eligibility & Licence Requirements

All drivers must be at least 21 years of age and present a valid, current, full Australian driver's licence or an approved international driver's licence (in English or accompanied by an official International Driving Permit) at pickup. Learner permits (L-plates) and suspended or expired licences are not accepted. Only drivers listed on the rental agreement are permitted to drive the vehicle.

2. Rental Payment & Refundable Security Bond

Full rental charges and a refundable security deposit must be paid prior to vehicle handover in Fraser Rise. The security bond is refunded in full following the return inspection, provided the vehicle is returned on time, in clean condition, free of new damage, and with no outstanding tolls or infringements.

3. Pickup, Return & Late Fees

Vehicles are collected from and returned to our Fraser Rise VIC 3336 depot (or your agreed local delivery address) at the scheduled times. Returns more than 60 minutes past the agreed return time without prior approval may incur an additional daily rental charge.

4. Fuel & Electric Vehicle Charging

Petrol and hybrid vehicles are supplied with a full tank of fuel and must be returned full (with the final fuel receipt if requested). Electric vehicles (such as the Tesla Model 3) must be returned with at least the same battery charge percentage as supplied at handover to avoid recharging fees.

5. Permitted Use, Roads & Care

Vehicles may only be driven on sealed, gazetted public roads within Victoria unless written permission is granted beforehand. Off-road driving, beach driving, racing, towing, sub-hiring, or commercial rideshare/delivery use without prior written consent is strictly prohibited. No smoking, vaping, or pets are allowed inside any vehicle; a professional detailing fee applies if smoke or pet odour/hair is present.

6. Tolls, Traffic Fines & Infringements

The renter is responsible for all road tolls (including CityLink and EastLink), speeding tickets, red-light cameras, and parking fines incurred during the rental period, plus any applicable administrative processing fees for nominating the driver.

7. Insurance, Damage & Breakdowns

All vehicles include standard third-party and comprehensive cover subject to the policy excess stated at handover. In the event of an accident, theft, or mechanical issue, you must notify HDCars4You immediately, obtain third-party details and a police report where required, and never admit liability at the scene. Damage caused by negligence, breach of these terms, overhead/underbody impact, or water submersion is not covered by standard excess limits.

8. Cancellations & Booking Extensions

Cancellations or date changes made at least 24 hours prior to pickup incur no fee. Late cancellations within 24 hours of pickup may forfeit one day's rental fee. Rental extensions must be requested at least 24 hours before the scheduled return time and are subject to vehicle availability.
