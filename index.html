What documents do I need to bring for pickup?

Please bring a valid, full Australian or international driver's licence along with a matching payment card for the rental fee and refundable security bond.

Do you offer local delivery around Fraser Rise?

Yes! You can collect your vehicle directly from our Fraser Rise VIC 3336 depot or select Fraser Rise Local Delivery when booking for drop-off in Fraser Rise, Caroline Springs, Taylors Hill, or Melton.

How does the refundable security bond work?

A standard refundable security deposit is held at pickup and released promptly once the vehicle is returned on time, undamaged, and at the same fuel or battery level.

Can I extend my rental if my plans change?

Absolutely. Just contact us by phone or email at least 24 hours before your scheduled return time and we will extend your booking if the vehicle is available.
